Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was a great Indian scientist and the 11th President of India. He is remembered for his contributions to India's space and defence programmes.

Early Life

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, in 1931. He came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve his dreams. He later became a scientist and inspired millions of students.

Important Achievement

One of his biggest achievements was his contribution to India's missile and space programmes. He played an important role in the development of India's missile technology. His dedication showed the power of determination and his hard work inspired many young people.

He was popularly known as the "Missile Man of India".
